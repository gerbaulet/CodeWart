Built for the way agents work

Commit by commit

See a branch's history since it forked, plus uncommitted work. Review one commit, a range, or everything at once, in any worktree.

Call-chain diagram

Every function the agent added, changed or deleted, drawn as a flow from caller to callee. Works in 15 languages with no extra setup.

Back to the agent

Comment on lines, then paste the review into the agent's tmux, WezTerm or kitty pane, or copy it as markdown.

Follows the agent

Watches the worktree and jumps to the newest change the moment the agent commits or saves a file.

Hide the noise

One click hides tests, lockfiles, generated code, renames and whitespace-only changes, so you read the logic.

Multi-repo features

Open a folder of repos as one group and jump between them on the same branch.

Get started

  1. Install and open the app. On macOS, if it says the app can't be opened, right-click it and choose Open once, or run: 
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Codewart.app
  2. Click "Install wart" in the top bar. This adds a small launcher to your PATH.
  3. Open any repo, worktree or folder of repos from your terminal: 
    wart .
